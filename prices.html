For residential foundations, Bamburi Tembo (32.5 MPa) is recommended as it offers the perfect balance of strength and cost-effectiveness.

For commercial or heavy-load structures (multi-storey buildings, industrial projects), choose Bamburi PowerMax (42.5 MPa) for its superior compressive strength and low heat of hydration.

Bamburi Fundi (22.5 MPa) is a specialized masonry cement designed for non-structural applications such as plastering, rendering, brick/block laying, and mortar works.

Bamburi Tembo (32.5 MPa) is a structural-grade all-purpose cement suitable for foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and general concrete works.

Key difference: Use Fundi for walls, plastering, and finishes. Use Tembo for anything structural that needs to bear weight.
